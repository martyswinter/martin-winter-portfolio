Overview

A fleet audit platform designed to support banks and leasing companies managing vehicle inspections across multiple locations.

The platform connects field auditors, coordinators and financial stakeholders by combining vehicle data, inspection results, AI-supported assessments and audit history into one digital workflow.

The Challenge

The platform needed to support multiple user groups with different goals and workflows.

Field auditors required a simple way to inspect vehicles, capture information and document damages on-site. Coordinators needed to manage assignments and monitor audit progress, while banks required a clear overview of vehicle condition, history and status.

My Contribution

As the UX/UI Designer, I focused on designing the main user flows, information structure and interface concepts across the platform.

My work included:

  • workflow design
  • Dashboard structure and data visualization
  • Vehicle detail views
  • Damage reporting flows
  • Case management and assignment flows
  • Status visualization and filtering concepts

I collaborated closely with the Product Owner to understand business requirements, structure complex information and translate processes into user-friendly solutions for development.

Design Process

I started by understanding the complete audit lifecycle and mapping how information moved between different users and stages of the process.

From there, I created user flows and structured the information hierarchy to ensure that each user could quickly access the information relevant to their role.

The designs were iterated through discussions with the Product Owner and refined into prototypes and implementation-ready interfaces for the development team.

Key Decisions

Prioritizing information hierarchy

The platform contained many different data points, from vehicle status and inspection results to historical audits and damage information.

I focused on presenting the most important information first and creating clear visual relationships between overview screens and detailed views.

Designing for multiple roles

Instead of creating one generic experience, the interface was structured around the different needs of auditors, coordinators and financial stakeholders.

Each role required different levels of detail, actions and information visibility.

The Outcome

The project established a structured digital workflow connecting vehicle inspections, audit management and fleet visibility within one platform.

The designed flows and interfaces provided a foundation for development and supported collaboration between business stakeholders, product teams and engineers.

Overview

An enterprise SaaS platform supporting automotive dealerships throughout the customer lifecycle — from initial lead analysis and customer contact to vehicle offers, contracts, delivery and after-sales processes.

The platform connected sales activities, customer information and operational workflows into one integrated environment used by dealership teams.

The Challenge

The platform covered multiple stages of the customer journey and supported different dealership roles with different goals and responsibilities.

Sales teams needed efficient ways to manage leads and customer interactions, while managers required structured overviews and visibility into ongoing activities.

My Contribution

As a UX/UI Designer, I focused on improving existing workflows and designing new product capabilities based on business needs, user feedback and process analysis.

My work included:

  • Analyzing existing customer and internal workflows
  • Identifying usability issues and proposing improved user flows
  • Designing new features for customer-facing and internal applications
  • Creating interface concepts, prototypes and implementation-ready designs
  • Improving overviews, lists, filtering and data presentation

Design Process

I started by understanding how users worked with the existing platform and where processes created unnecessary complexity.

Key Decisions

Simplifying complex business processes
